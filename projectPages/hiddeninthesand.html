Hidden in the Sand Music Video

<Back to main page>

In a video production class that I took a few years ago, I aspired to push my boundaries with my creations. This music video for Tally Hall's "Hidden in the Sand" is a prime example of this, as over the course of a weekend I taught myself to use Blender (a 3D modeling software) and EbSynth (a rotoscoping software) in order to achieve my vision for the project. Although the result is admittedly not incredibly polished, it serves as an exemplary proof of my passion to always push my limits and grow my skillset when creating.
