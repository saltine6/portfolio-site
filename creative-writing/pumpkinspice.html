Pumpkin Spice

Written 11/21/24

There are three things that a modern human needs to comfortable exist within our society: Shelter, clothing, and food. Because of their omnipresent nature, we as individuals feel an innate need to personalize these aspects of our existense, to utilize them as vehicles of self-expression. What one wears expresses their intended persona just as the decorations in their house reveal their definition of "home". The third in this trifecta is often overlooked; How can the food one eats give an insight to their subjective reality?

There's the obvious: it tells you what flavors they prefer, umami, sweet, savory; But the analysis can run deeper. Someone's preferences in food reveal details of their upbringing, their culture, even their current mood. This begs the question: what does Pumpkin Spice say about an individual? Depending on the form of pumpkin spice, the answer will vary, whether it's coffee, donuts, or otherwise. However, some generalizations can be made. One who enjoys pumpkin spice likely appreciates the colder seasons. The turning leaves present an opportunity to hunker down with a blanket and a good book. The distinctive flavor of cinnamon suggests a distinctive individual, or at least one who wishes to be.

While generalizations can be made based on either clothing, shelter, or food, truly effective analysis, that which evolves from the purely speculative, emerges from a joined consideration of the trifecta. Someone purchasing a pumpkin spice coffee while wearing dark clothes, for example, before going home to their firelit living room represents an entirely different story than the next person in line, picking up a box of pumpkin spice donuts for a vibrant house party as they readjust their polka dotted scarf and red mittens. In the end, the true beauty of pumpkin spice comes from its adaptability. Everyone wants to express themselves differently, and pumpkin spice can transform itself to everyone's needs without ever changing.
