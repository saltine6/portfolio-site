The Box

1/8/25

I stare at the box and the box stares back, sensing my unease. It calls to me. Murmurs of the unknown cloud my mind. It urges me to unveil its secrets. I open the box.

Inside of the box I find another, slightly smaller, box. The box gets louder, pounding, threatening to burst my head from within as the pressure grows. I open the box.

Inside of the second box is another, slightly smaller, box. I don't fight the urge anymore. I open the box.

I am inside the box. I am urging a bystander to open the box, pleading that they set me free. I am insistent. They open the box.

Suddenly, I am standing back on the street. I watch as a small, black box falls from a woman's coat pocket and lands by my feet.
